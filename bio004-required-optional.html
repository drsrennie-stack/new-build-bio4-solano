What do I have to do, and what is my choice?

Your grade comes from five things. The study tools are up to you.

Each TBL is worth 40 points: 30 from your iRAT and 10 from your team's tRAT. From Module 3 on, you earn the 10 team points when your own iRAT is 70% or higher. See What is the 70% rule?

Which forms do I have to sign?

Two, both in Canvas.

  • The digital device agreement, before your first lab.
  • The AI Honor Contract, before you turn in any graded work.

What should I do before every class?

This part is not graded, but it is how you get ready for TBLs.

  • Read the notes.
  • Draw them on your note sheets.
  • Watch the concept video.
  • Read your lab structure list.

The pre-work worksheets are optional, and I recommend them.

Do I have to use every study tool?

No. Pick the ones that work for you.

  • For lecture: recall cards, Mastery OS, brain dump practice, Kahoots and practice exams
  • For lab: Loops, the Digital Atlas, lab sprints, histology help and cadaver videos
  • With other people: Study With Me and tutoring

You do not need to use every study tool, but you do need to master every competency.

Can I earn extra credit?

Yes, in two ways. There is no other extra credit.
