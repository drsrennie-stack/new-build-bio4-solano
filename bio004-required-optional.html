What do I have to do, and what is my choice?

Your grade comes from five things. The study tools are up to you.

Each TBL is worth 40 points, 30 from your iRAT and 10 from your team's tRAT. From Module 3 on, you only earn the 10 team points if your own iRAT is 70% or higher. To learn more, see What is the 70% rule?

Which forms do I have to sign?

You need to sign two forms in Canvas: the digital device agreement before your first lab, and the AI Honor Contract before you turn in any graded work.

What should I do before every class?

This part isn't graded, but it's how you get ready for TBLs. Before each class, read the notes, draw them on your note sheets, watch the concept video and read your lab structure list.

The pre-work worksheets are optional, but I recommend them.

Do I have to use every study tool?

No. Pick the ones that work for you.

  • For lecture: recall cards, Mastery OS, brain dump practice, Kahoots and practice exams
  • For lab: Loops, the Digital Atlas, lab sprints, histology help and cadaver videos
  • With other people: Study With Me and tutoring

You don't need to use every study tool, but you do need to master every competency.

Can I earn extra credit?
