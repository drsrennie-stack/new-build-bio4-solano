You are not behind

Dr. Sharilyn Rennie · Aug 24, 2026 · 2 min read

Somewhere around week two, almost every student in this course has the same quiet thought: everyone else gets it, and I am behind. I want you to know two things about that thought. First, nearly everyone in the room is having it. Second, it is measuring the wrong thing.

The syllabus is a map, not a race. A map tells you where things are and how they connect. It does not tell you that you have failed because you are standing at one point on it instead of another. Where you are right now is a place to start from, not a verdict.

What to do with the feeling

Do not try to argue with it, and do not obey it either. Behind is not a feeling you fix by studying twelve hours on a Saturday. It is a feeling you shrink by doing one specific thing today: one loop, one page of your tissue chart, one brain dump. Small and real beats big and imaginary every single time.

You do not need to catch up to everyone. You need to be a little further along than you were yesterday.

And if the week has genuinely gotten away from you, come talk to me. Not because you are in trouble. Because that is what office hours are for, and because the students who ask for a hand early are the ones who end the term strong.
