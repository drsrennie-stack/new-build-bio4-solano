Brain dump practice

Pick what you want to be asked about, spin, and get one prompt. Set your own clock, write it on paper with nothing open, then check yourself against the key points.

What you score here goes to your weak spot board, so the app stops guessing what you need.

Step 1

What should it ask you about?

Pick as many as you like. One module to drill it, or several to be caught off guard, which is closer to what an exam does. Modules marked not covered yet are ahead of where class is, so they start off. Switch them on if you want to work ahead.

Step 2

How long do you want?

In class it is five minutes. On your own, longer is fine while you are learning the format. The clock is there to stop you drifting, not to fail you.

minutes, up to 45
Step 3

Spin for your prompt

Ten prompts go on the wheel each time, drawn from what you picked. If none of them appeal, spin again and it reloads with ten different ones.

Your prompt

Your brain dump prompt

Paper, closed notes, closed book, phone away. Do not type this. Write it, draw it, label it. Organized and labeled counts. A scatter of words does not.

Step 4

Before you look, how did that go?

Answer this before you see the key points. The gap between what you thought and what you actually got is the most useful thing on this page.

Step 5

What the answer should have had

Tick every point you actually got down on paper. Be honest with yourself here, a generous tick only hides the gap from you.
