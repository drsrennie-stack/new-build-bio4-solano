    BIO 004 · The graded part

    Now fix the why.

    Familiar is not the same as known

    Familiar means the answer looks right once you see it. Known means you can produce it with the page blank and the options hidden. Re-reading, highlighting, and watching videos build familiarity; they feel like studying because everything you look at seems recognizable. The exam does not ask whether you recognize the answer. It asks you to produce it.

    Only forced retrieval builds knowledge: blank-page brain dumps, recall cards, gap finders, saying it out loud with the notes closed. If most of your misses land in category 2 below, that is your diagnosis, and it is the most fixable one on the list.
