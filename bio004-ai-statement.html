AI in this course · BIO 004 · Fall 2026

Is this an AI course?

No. I've heard some of you say you'd rather have a regular course than an AI course, so I want to clear that up. This is an in-person anatomy course that I designed and teach. I did use AI to help build some of your study tools, and I want you to know exactly how.

Who built this course

I've been building this course for over 12 years.

01Hundreds of hoursThat's how much of my time has gone into this course, from the competencies and notes to the note sheets, lab structure lists and exams.
02Built over 12 yearsI've taught this material and kept improving it for more than 12 years. None of it showed up overnight.
03Taught by meI recorded your video lectures, and I'm in the room with you in class and in lab.
How this course works

How you'll learn in this course

  1. 01
    You watch my video lectures before class.There's a full semester of them, and I recorded them for this course. Watching first means you've already met the topic when you walk in.
  2. 02
    Class is active learning.We spend class time using what you learned: TBLs, working problems with your team, and live lecture on the harder concepts.
  3. 03
    Lab is hands-on.You work with microscope slides, models and human donors.
  4. 04
    Exams are in person.You take your lecture exams and lab practicals in the room.

Your use of AI

What about your use of AI?

Can I use AI to study?

Yes, and I encourage it. Try the Teach It to an AI feature in Mastery OS. It opens your own free ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini account with a quiz prompt ready to go. Explaining a concept to something that keeps asking you questions is a good way to find out whether you really know it.

I'm happy to teach you how to use AI to help you learn, not to do things for you. Just ask me in class or at office hours.

Can I use AI on graded work?
  • You can only use AI on assignments marked as AI assignments.
  • You can't use AI on exams or on any graded part of a TBL.
  • If graded work is rated above 25% AI or plagiarism, it gets an automatic zero.
  • If you're not sure whether a tool counts, ask me before you turn your work in.
Where are the full rules?

They're in the AI Honor Contract in Canvas. Sign it before you turn in any graded work.

What if I don't want to use tools built with AI?

That's completely up to you. The study tools are optional. You're welcome to make your own from your notes, note sheets and competencies, like flashcards, drawings or practice questions.

It takes more of your time, but it works. What matters is that you master every competency.
