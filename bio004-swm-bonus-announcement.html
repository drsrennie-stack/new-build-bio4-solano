Study With Me: Bonus Week

I want to get more of you using Study With Me this week, so I am adding a little extra incentive.

Bonus for this week only

  • Set up and host a Study With Me: earn your regular Scholar Point time plus 1 bonus point per hour.
  • Attend a Study With Me: earn your regular Scholar Point time plus 0.5 bonus point per hour.
  • This week's Study With Me cap is increased to 5 hours.

The goal is simple: find some people and study together. You do not need to be an expert or create a formal tutoring session. Pick something you need to learn, invite your classmates, and work on it together.

What counts as a Study With Me?

Study With Me must happen in one of two ways.

Option 1: Zoom. The session must be held on Zoom and recorded so participation can be verified if needed.

Option 2: In person at the ASTC. You may meet in person during ASTC hours. A tutor must be able to verify who attended, how long each person participated, and that the group was actively studying rather than simply hanging out or chatting.

In either format, you need to be actively participating for the time you claim. You don't have to be intensely studying every second, but this should be a real study session.

Everyone must be invited

Study With Me is open to everyone in the class. If you are hosting, you must post your session on the Study With Me board so everyone has the opportunity to participate.

You can absolutely plan a session with a few classmates first. Just put it on the board so someone else who wants to study has the opportunity to join you.

If it isn't posted on the Study With Me board, it doesn't count for Scholar Points.

Sign up if you want credit

If you plan to attend and want Scholar Points, you need to sign up for the session. At the end of the session, the host should send me a list of who signed up, who actually attended, and how long each person participated.

For ASTC sessions, the tutor should verify attendance and participation. For Zoom sessions, the recording provides verification if needed.

Please don't sign up, appear for a few minutes, and claim the entire session. Scholar Points are for the time you actually participate.

What should you do?

Whatever helps you learn. Here are some ideas:

  • Play Loops together.
  • Work on the histology tissue chart together.
  • Do practice questions and discuss why you chose your answers.
  • Do a brain dump independently, then come back together and compare what you remembered and what you missed.
  • Play Kahoot. Use mine or make your own.
  • Discuss the pre-work and work through things you didn't understand.
  • Quiz each other using lab models, tissues, structures, or current material.
  • Teach each other. Try explaining a concept without your notes and let the group help fill in the gaps.

Those are suggestions, not requirements. If you have another productive way to study together, use it.

Study With Me is not tutoring

You don't need to know more than everyone else to host. The host is simply the person willing to say, "We're studying Tuesday at 6. Who wants to join?"

You can all have questions. You can all struggle with something. You can figure it out together. Someone just needs to get it started.

This week, I'm giving you extra credit for being that person.

Post it. Invite everyone. Sign up. Show up. Actually study. You can earn up to 5 hours this week, so get this on the books!
