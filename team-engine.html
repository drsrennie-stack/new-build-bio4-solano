Team Formation Engine.

Two ways to build instructor-formed TBL teams, one place. Quick Deal takes a pasted roster and spreads clinical experience evenly in seconds, which is the day-one method. Survey Balance takes the Google Form CSV and balances background, leadership, tech comfort, work hours, social mix, and meeting windows, with a transparency report. Both name teams the same way and put the result on the projector. Everything runs on this device.

How the teams were built (transparency report)

Students are sorted by background strength and dealt across teams in a snake pattern so every team gets a comparable spread. The tool then swaps members to make sure each team has an organizer and a tech-comfortable member, to even out work-hours load, to mix introverts and extroverts, to share at least one meeting window, and to avoid leaving anyone as the lone member of a group.
