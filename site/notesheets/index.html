How we use them

  1. Print in color if you can. Black and white works; the atlas has the color version of every photo.
  2. In lecture, each structure comes up on the screen one at a time. Write the name on the numbered line, then fill the structure notes card as its lines appear.
  3. Bone map pages: shaded rust is the origin, shaded blue is the insertion. Name each one.
  4. Vessel and nerve maps: the dashed lines are landmarks (inguinal ligament, knee, neck of the fibula, the malleoli). Number each segment on the list.
  5. Missed a line? The atlas has the photo and the name. The explanations are given in class.
