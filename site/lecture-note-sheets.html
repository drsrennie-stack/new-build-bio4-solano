How we use them

  • Each structure comes up on the screen one at a time. Write the name on the numbered line, then fill the structure card as its lines appear.
  • Muscle cards show the bones underneath: shaded rust is the origin, shaded blue is the insertion. Name each one on the lines above.
  • Vessel and nerve maps: the dashed lines are landmarks. Number each segment on the list.
  • Missed a name? Every photo is in the Digital Atlas with its pins. The explanations are given in class.
