Section 1 · Multiple choice

30 questions, 1 point each. Pick the single best answer.

Section 2 · True or false, with the bonus

10 statements, 1 point each. If you mark a statement false, a box opens: type the one word that should replace the bold term to make it true. Each correct replacement is a bonus point, and this is the only bonus on the exam.

Section 3 · Matching

Two sets, 1 point per match. Each description is used exactly once.

Section 4 · Confidence questions

Scored exactly like your tRAT: each question gives you 4 points to place across the answers. All 4 on one answer if you are certain, split them if you are not. You earn whatever you placed on the right answer.
