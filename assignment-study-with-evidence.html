What to do

  1. 1

    Read through the slides

    Go through all 24 slides. Use the Next button or the arrow keys. On the Lab Sprint slide, tap each round to open it. Pay attention to the six patterns and find the one that sounds most like you.

    Open the slides
  2. 2

    Complete the metacognition activity

    Three parts. Answer every box. In Part 2 you close everything and do one competency or structure set from memory, then check it. Pick something from the last few days (respiratory, if you have started it) or from an earlier unit.

    • If you draw on paper, write "on paper" in the box and scan or photograph the drawing to add to your submission.
    • Your answers save on the device you are using, so finish on the same phone or computer you started on.
    Open the activity
  3. 3

    Print it as a PDF

    At the bottom of the activity, click Print or save as PDF. In the print window, choose Save as PDF as the printer (on a phone, use Share and then Save to Files or Print). Name the file with your last name, for example Rennie-metacognition.pdf.

    Check the PDF before you submit. Your typed answers should show in every box.

  4. 4

    Submit the PDF in Canvas

    Upload the PDF to this assignment. If you drew Part 2 on paper, add the photo or scan as a second file in the same submission.

How it is graded

Complete

Every box in all three parts has a real answer. "I don't know" is not an answer. "I could not remember the valves" is.

Specific

Part 1 gives numbers and activities, not "studied a lot." Part 2 names the exact competency or structures and shows what you got right, missed, and got wrong.

One real change

Part 3 names one change, one resource, a day and time, and both checks: one right after, one on a later day.

Before office hours

If you want help with studying or an exam after doing this, fill out the office hours preparation form and bring this activity with you. Look up anything you can look up first, and bring what you found.
