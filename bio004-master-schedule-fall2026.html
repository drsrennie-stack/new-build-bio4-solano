Read this before you print anything

Exam 4 is on a Monday for Class 1. Every other Class 1 exam is a Wednesday.

Class 1 sits all of Exam 4 on Monday November 16, lecture exam in the 12:30 block and lab practical in the 2:00 block. The date comes from the department lab schedule, which is also what your setup and takedown duty is built around.

Every exam in every section has both parts on the same day. Classes 2 and 3 sit Exam 4 on Tuesday November 17. The only thing unusual about Exam 4 is the weekday, so say that out loud to Class 1 more than once.
