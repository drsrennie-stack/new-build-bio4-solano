Two views, same file
  Lecture page: all slides stacked as cards, everything revealed, for students to review after class.
  Present (button top right, or "Present from here" on any slide): one slide fills the screen, facts hidden.
    Photo slide: each click (right arrow, space, or the > button) drops the next numbered pin with its box and leader line;
      the next click reveals that structure's facts as bullets; then the next pin.
    Fill-in slide: click reveals the next stem, the next click reveals the answer. Same stems as the note sheet.
    Click any box or card to reveal it out of order.
    Bottom bar: back, count, next, Reveal all, Reset, Facts hidden/shown toggle, ?, Back to lecture. Esc also exits.
    Keys: left/right, space, Enter, Backspace, N (skip slide), A (reveal all), R (reset), Home, End, ?.
  #s12 in the URL opens the lecture page scrolled to slide 12.

Facts come from the Module 4 lecture notes packet (competencies 97 to 108 and 127). Photos: BIO 004 Digital Atlas.
