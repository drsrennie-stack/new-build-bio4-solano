What is a TBL, and how do I get ready?

These are the questions students ask most about TBLs.

What is a TBL?

TBL stands for Team-Based Learning. It's a short quiz you take twice, first on your own and then with your team.

It checks that you came to class ready, and TBLs are 30% of your grade.

What happens during a TBL?

  1. iRAT (individual quiz)You answer the questions on your own.
  2. tRAT (team quiz)Your team answers the same questions together and agrees on each answer.
  3. AppealsIf your team thinks a question was unclear or the key is wrong, you can challenge it.
  4. ApplicationYour team works through problems on the same material. This part isn't graded, but come ready to talk it through.

Can I use my phone or laptop?

No. You can't use any devices from the start of the iRAT to the end of the tRAT.

What is on a TBL?

Anything we've covered so far in the module can be on a TBL. The topic on the calendar tells you what the TBL focuses on, but questions can also come from earlier in the module.

Are there TBLs in lab?

No, lab TBLs are no longer part of the course. In lab, you work on the models first and then the cadaver.

How do I get ready for a TBL?

  1. Read the notesGo one section at a time.
  2. Draw it on your note sheetsDraw from your notes in one color.
  3. Watch the videosFix your drawings in a second color as you watch.
  4. Check the competenciesYour class schedule lists them for each day.

12.5%

Only 12.5% of your course grade depends on your team. That's the tRAT part of each TBL plus the lab quizzes. The other 87.5% comes from your own work.

New since Module 3

What is the 70% rule?

70%

To get your team's tRAT points on a TBL, you need at least 70% on your own iRAT first. If you score 70% or higher, you get your iRAT score plus your team's tRAT score. If you score below 70%, you keep your iRAT score, but you don't get the team points for that TBL.

This started after Exam 2, so it applies to every TBL from Module 3 on. The best way to stay above 70% is to do the prep before every TBL.

Why do I need 70% to get the team points?

70% is a C, the lowest passing grade in this course. If you score below that, you don't know the material well enough yet to help your team, and you end up relying on your teammates for the answers.

The tRAT works because everyone at the table brings something. Your team needs you to explain why an answer is right, catch the ones that are wrong, and help the group decide. The team points are for that, so you earn them by coming prepared. It's also fair to the classmates who did the prep.

It keeps your grade honest, too. If you scored below 70% and still got the team points, your grade would say you're more ready than you are, and you'd find out on the exam, where your team can't help you. It's better to see it now, while there's still time to fix it.

You still take part in the tRAT and learn from the discussion. You just don't get the team points that day, and nothing comes off your iRAT score.

Does the 70% rule change the grading in the syllabus?

No. TBLs are still 30% of your grade, and each TBL is still worth 40 points, 30 from your iRAT and 10 from the tRAT. The rule only decides who earns the 10 team points.

I announced it before it started, and it only counts from Module 3 on. TBLs from Modules 1 and 2 are scored the way they always were.

Can my team score be lower than my own score?

Yes, sometimes. If you had the right answer but couldn't convince your team, the team can still pick the wrong one. Your iRAT score is yours, but the tRAT score is based on what your team decides together.

That's part of the skill. In the tRAT, knowing the answer isn't enough. You need to be able to explain why it's right.

Give your reason along with your answer. "I think it's B because the tibia is the medial bone of the leg" can change minds, while "I think it's B" usually won't. Point to the structure, where it sits, or the competency it comes from. If your team still disagrees and you think the key is wrong, file an appeal together.

Over a whole term, teams that talk things through usually score higher than any one member does alone.

What if I miss a TBL?

There are no make-ups for TBLs. Sometimes I give an unannounced TBL, but there's no promise there will be one. When there is, your lowest TBL score drops.

When are the TBLs?

Every TBL is on your class schedule in Canvas. Check it each week so a TBL never catches you by surprise.
