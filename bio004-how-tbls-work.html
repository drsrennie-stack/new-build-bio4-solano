What is a TBL, and how do I get ready?

Find your question below. Each answer is short.

What is a TBL?

TBL stands for Team-Based Learning. It is a short quiz you take twice: first on your own, then with your team.

It checks that you came to class ready. TBLs are 30% of your grade.

What happens during a TBL?

  1. iRAT (individual quiz)You answer the questions on your own.
  2. tRAT (team quiz)Your team answers the same questions together and agrees on each answer.
  3. AppealsYour team can challenge a question it thinks was unclear or keyed wrong.
  4. ApplicationYour team works problems on the same material. Not graded, but come ready to talk it through.

Can I use my phone or laptop?

No. No devices from the start of the iRAT to the end of the tRAT.

What is on a TBL?

  • Anything taught so far in the module.
  • The topic on the calendar is the focus, not the limit.

Are there TBLs in lab?

No. Lab TBLs are no longer part of the course. Lab days run on the models first, then the cadaver.

How do I get ready for a TBL?

  1. Read the notesOne section at a time.
  2. Draw it on your note sheetsFrom your notes, in one color.
  3. Watch the videosCorrect your drawings in a second color.
  4. Check the competenciesYour class schedule lists them for each day.

How much does my team affect my grade?

12.5%

Team work decides 12.5% of your course grade: the tRAT part of each TBL plus lab quizzes.

The other 87.5% is you alone.

What if I miss a TBL?

  • There are no make-ups.
  • Sometimes I give an unannounced TBL. There is no promise there will be one.
  • When there is one, your lowest TBL score drops.
