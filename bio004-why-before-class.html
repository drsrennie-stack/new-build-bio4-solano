Why you learn before class

You meet each topic on your own first, so class time goes to the hard part, with help right there.

Before class

  • Read the notes for the day.
  • Draw it on your note sheets.
  • Watch the concept video.
  • Read your lab structure list.

In class

  • Lecture on the concepts that matter most, with check-in questions.
  • TBLs and problems with your team.
  • Finding structures on the models and the cadaver.

Why this order

The hard part of anatomy is not hearing a name for the first time. It is finding that structure on a skull with four other bones around it and no labels.

That part goes better with me and your team in the room, so that is what class time is for.

Every lecture is still taught live. The videos get you ready for class. They do not replace it.

If the video did not make sense

  • Use the video's chapter list to jump back to that concept.
  • Bring your question to class. That is what class is for.
  • Office hours: the 30 minutes before each class, in our room.
  • Ask in the Virtual Office, where the whole class sees the answer.
