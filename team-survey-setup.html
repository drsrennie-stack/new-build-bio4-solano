Team Survey Setup.

How the pieces fit: students open team-survey.html from a QR code or the pop-up on their class page, answer twelve questions one at a time, and the page posts their answers into a Google Form you own, which lands in a Google Sheet. You download that Sheet as CSV, drop it into the Team Formation Engine (Survey Balance tab), pick the section, and get team cards, a class list, and a printable roster. Connecting it takes about ten minutes, once. Page two of this document is a paper copy of the survey for anyone without a phone.

Step 1 · about 5 minutes

Reuse your existing form

You already have TBL Team Survey in Google Forms, and it matches what the engine expects. The survey page is already pointed at it (its FORM_ID is filled in). Open the form in the editor and make these small edits, then move to Step 2 for the entry IDs.

  1. Add one question at the top: Which class are you in? Multiple choice: Class 1 · Mon/Wed / Class 2 · Tue/Thu morning / Class 3 · Tue/Thu evening. This is what lets one form serve all three classes; the engine filters by it. If you would rather keep three separate forms, make two copies and set a different FORM_ID per section instead.
  2. On On a team, I prefer to... add a fourth choice: Encourage people and keep the mood positive. Everything else in that question stays.
  3. In Settings, make sure Collect email addresses is off and Limit to 1 response is off, so students never have to sign in.
  4. The Gender and English questions can stay or go; the survey page never sends them. Removing them keeps the promise on the page simple.
  5. Optional, if you want the richer data: add these four, in any order, all not required. How much anatomy or physiology coursework have you had before this class? (Multiple choice: None yet, this is my first / Some, in high school or a general biology class / I have completed an anatomy or physiology course before). Which of these describe you? (Checkboxes: I am reliable about showing up and following through / I ask good questions / I am comfortable speaking up in a group / I notice when someone is lost and help them catch up / I like explaining things to other people / I keep the group on task / I bring calm when things get stressful). What matters most to you on a team? (Short answer). Anything you want me to know? (Short answer). Skip any you do not want; the page only sends what has an entry ID.
  6. If the form does not already have a linked Sheet: Responses tab, green Sheets icon, Create a new spreadsheet.
Step 2 · about 3 minutes

Get the entry IDs

  1. In the form editor, click the three-dot menu (top right) and choose Get pre-filled link.
  2. Type any answer into every question, including the checkbox ones, then click Get link and Copy link.
  3. Paste the link into a text editor. It contains pieces like entry.1234567890=..., one per question, in the same order as the form. Those numbers are the entry IDs.
  4. The FORM_ID is the long string in the form's own URL between /d/e/ and /viewform.
Step 3 · about 2 minutes

Connect the survey page

  1. Open team-survey.html in the repo. Near the top of the script the FORM_ID is already set to your form, and below it is an ENTRY block with one line per question, each with a comment naming the question it belongs to.
  2. Paste each entry ID between the quotes on its line. Leave a line blank for any question you did not add to the form; the page skips blank ones.
  3. Commit and push. Open the page on your phone, answer it once as a test, and confirm the row lands in the Sheet. Then delete that test row.

Until FORM_ID is filled in, the page still works: at the end it offers an "Email my answers" button that opens a message to your Solano address with the answers as one CSV line, so nothing a student does tonight is lost.

Data retention

What students are told, and what to do about it

The survey page tells students their answers live in your course form, are used only to form teams, and that the file is deleted once teams are final, at the close of add/drop. That wording is on purpose: waiting until add/drop closes gives you the data if a late add or a run of drops means a team needs rebuilding in week two, and it still keeps a firm, dated promise. Deleting the moment teams are announced would leave you rebuilding blind.

  1. After the last team change of the add/drop window, open the linked Sheet and File, Move to trash, then empty the trash in Drive.
  2. Open the form, Responses tab, three-dot menu, Delete all responses. Then either delete the form or keep the empty shell for next term.
  3. Delete any downloaded CSVs from your laptop's Downloads folder. The Team Formation Engine keeps nothing once the tab closes.
  4. Keep the final rosters in Canvas groups only. That is the system of record, and it is where a roster belongs.

If you would rather promise same-day deletion, the only cost is that a week-two rebuild would need students to retake the survey. The retention wording lives in one sentence in team-survey.html and can be changed either way.

On the day

From Sheet to teams

  1. In the linked Sheet: File, Download, Comma-separated values.
  2. Open the Team Formation Engine from the faculty hub, Survey Balance tab, load the CSV. The column mapping fills itself from the question titles.
  3. Choose the Section, set team size, Generate. Re-roll if you want a different arrangement.
  4. Show on projector for the announcement. Print for the roster and the alphabetical class list. Download CSV for the Canvas groups.
  5. Anyone who took the paper survey: type their row into the Sheet before you download, or add them by hand to the smallest team afterward.
