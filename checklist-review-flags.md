## The rule on function, set August 9

Basic function is appropriate on any anatomy item. What stays out is physiology
that runs as a sequence, the multi-step mechanisms. Anatomical sequences, tracing
blood through the heart or CSF from the choroid plexus, are routes through
structures rather than mechanisms, and they stay. The rule is recorded in the
header of `draw-checklists.js`.

## How to change one

Open `draw-checklists.js`, find the `code:` above, and edit the strings inside
`items: [ ... ]`. One structure or one labelled relationship per string, no
trailing period. To change which competencies a drawing scores against, edit its
`comps: [ ... ]`. Nothing else needs touching.
