What Spiral Rounds is, and where it went

It did not go anywhere. Every sprint page has it, near the bottom. Each category is broken into sets of about ten to twelve structures, and you take a set through five rounds that each ask a bit more of you than the last: someone names them all out loud, then you name them, then you name them without the labels, and so on.

That is the part that actually gets them into your head. Reading the list once does very little. Do the rounds with your group, tick the sprint off here when you can name the whole thing cold, and come back to it a few days later.
