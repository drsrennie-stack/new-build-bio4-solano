Quick Teams.

Paste your roster, mark who has hands-on healthcare experience, and deal balanced random teams in one click. Clinical students are spread evenly first, then everyone else is shuffled in, so no team ends up stacked. Everything runs on this device and disappears when you close the tab.

Step 1

Paste the roster

One student per line. Put an asterisk after anyone with hands-on healthcare experience, like Maria Lopez *. That is the only mark the tool needs. Names never leave this page.

Step 2

Your teams

The clinical marks show only on your screen; projector mode hides them so nobody is singled out in front of the room. How it works: clinical students are shuffled and dealt one per team, round-robin, then everyone else is shuffled and dealt the same way. Leftover students fill the smallest teams, so sizes never differ by more than one. Re-deal any time you want a different arrangement.
