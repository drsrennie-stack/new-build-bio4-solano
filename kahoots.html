Instructor note

Adding or updating a Kahoot

Open this file, find the KAHOOTS list near the top of the script, and paste the Kahoot link (the kahoot.it challenge link or the create.kahoot.it share link) into the entry's url. The tile flips from "link coming" to a Play button by itself, and the QR builds from the same link. Add a new entry the same way; module can be 1 to 5.

{ module: 2, name: "Kahoot Review: Bone and Bone Details", url: "https://kahoot.it/challenge/...", blurb: "The week 7 class review." }
