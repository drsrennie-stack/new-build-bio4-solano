Study tools

Five apps, yours to use

These are the practice tools from BIO 004 Human Anatomy. They are free, they need no account, and you can use them whether or not you are in the course.

Scan a code with your phone camera. Nothing to install.

  • Your study engine

    Mastery OS

    Spaced recall that brings a card back exactly when you are about to forget it. It tracks what is weak, builds the day, and keeps the evidence of what you have actually learned.

    Best on a laptop. Your progress saves on the device you use.

    Scan to open

  • Fast visual practice

    Lab Loops

    Thirty-nine image loops. A structure appears, you name it, the answer follows. Built for the ten minutes between other things.

    Works well on a phone. Your misses count toward your weak areas.

    Scan to open

  • Turn it around

    Digital Atlas

    Explore structures in three dimensions. Rotate them, take layers off, and see how things sit against each other before you ever meet them on a model.

    Phone or laptop. Give it a moment to load the first time.

    Scan to open

  • Write it from memory

    Brain dumps

    Three levels per topic. Pick a prompt, write everything you know on paper, then check your answer against the key.

    Paper and a pen. The timer is on the page.

    Scan to open

  • A simulated exam

    Practice exam

    A fresh 100 point paper in the real format. Pick the topics and the difficulty, take it, and see the score with the reasoning.

    None of it counts toward your grade.
