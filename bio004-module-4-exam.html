How this exam works. Answer every section the way you would on paper, then press Submit exam at the bottom. Nothing is revealed until you submit, so treat it closed book. Your work saves in this browser as you go, so a refresh will not cost you anything.

True and False. One term in every statement is bold and underlined, and that is the term you correct. There is no blank to fill in. Decide true or false, and if it is false work out the term that belongs there instead. After you submit, the correct term appears beside your answer and you claim a quarter point for each one you had right.

Multiple choice, confidence based. Each question gives you four confidence points to place wherever you want across the four options. All four on one answer when you know it. Split them two and two, or three and one, when you are down to a couple of options. Your score is the points you placed on the correct answer times 0.25.

Matching. Choose the best match for each term. Every bank holds extra options that match nothing.

Brain dump, choose one. Four prompts, you answer one. Mark the one you want counted, write everything you can, and after you submit a checklist opens so you can award yourself credit for every element you included.

Your grade. Submitting gives you a percentage, a letter grade, a breakdown by section, and a topic by topic report showing where you are solid and where you are not, so the number you see is close to the one you would earn on exam day.
