Day One Run Sheet.

Everything for the first class meeting of each section, in order: what to do tonight, how to set up the podium, the minute-by-minute run of show, the keys you will actually press, and what to do afterward. Page two is a printable sign-in sheet that doubles as the roster for team building.

Tonight, before Monday

Setup, about 30 minutes

  1. Push the repo. Make sure these are in the repo root and pushed: slides-course-introduction.html, faculty.html, team-engine.html, team-formation-plan.html, bio004-team-charter-worksheet.html, plus the updated welcome.html and tools-and-links.html. Once the slides file is live, the QR on the title slide resolves.
  2. Test the QR. Open slides-course-introduction.html on GitHub Pages and scan the code on slide 1 with your phone. If it does not open the slides, tell me the live URL and I will regenerate it.
  3. Print. Team Charter Worksheet: one per student plus five spares, per section. Sign-in sheet (page two of this document): one or two per section depending on headcount. Print single-sided.
  4. Prep the roster paste. From your Canvas People page, copy each section's names into a plain text note, one name per line, one note per section. This is what you will paste into Team Formation Engine; you will add the asterisks in class from the sign-in sheet.
  5. Bookmark faculty.html on your laptop, and if you present from the podium computer, put the URL on a sticky note. Everything else opens from there.
  6. Charge and pack: laptop, HDMI or USB-C adapter, phone (for charter photos), your master notes, and the printed pages above.
Ten minutes before class

Podium setup

  1. Open faculty.html. Pick the section in the pull-down. That choice follows you into every course page.
  2. Open four tabs from the hub, in this order: Intro Slides, Team Formation Engine, Brain Dump Selector, Course Page. Leave Intro Slides as the active tab.
  3. In the Team Formation Engine, open the Survey Balance tab and have the Google Sheet open in another tab. As a fallback, the Quick Deal tab with paste tonight's roster for this section, set team size (6 for most rooms), and pick National parks. Do not deal yet.
  4. Put the projector on the Intro Slides tab, click Present, confirm slide 1 fills the screen with the QR readable from the back. Press T once and back to confirm the clock appears and hides. Press Esc to exit until students arrive.
  5. Set the sign-in sheet and a pen at the door with the worksheets stacked beside it. Students take one of each on the way in.
Run of show

First class, about two hours of the block

0:00DoorStudents sign in and check the hands-on patient care box if it applies, take a worksheet, sit anywhere. Slide 1 is on screen so early arrivals scan the QR.
0:05Slides 1–2Welcome and who you are. Open the three career boxes one at a time with the arrow key. Say: every choice in this class has a reason, and I am going to show you the reasons.
0:12Slides 3–6The evidence, the full lecture, why the sheet comes before the video, how memory works. Let the boxes zoom one per arrow press; the red keyword line is what you speak to, the small print is there if someone asks. On slide 3, say the collegial line out loud: there is great teaching in every format.
0:30Slides 7–9How TBL works, why teams, then the survey slide with the three QR codes. Students who already did it from the class-page pop-up just wait; everyone else has three minutes on their phones. Hand the paper survey (page two of Team Survey Setup) to anyone without a phone.
0:38Build the teamsWhile students finish, open the linked Google Sheet, File, Download, CSV, then the Team Formation Engine tab, Survey Balance, load the file, pick this section, Generate. Type any paper surveys into the Sheet first if there are only a few; otherwise add those students by hand to the smallest teams. Glance at the balance, then Show on projector. Say: I built these teams from your survey so every team starts with a similar mix. You are with these people all term. Find your team and sit together now. Give two minutes to move.
Fallback if the form is not connected yet: use the Quick Deal tab with the sign-in sheet asterisks instead. Same announcement.
0:45Slides 9–10What a week feels like, how the grade is built. Point at the grade bar; it tells the story on its own.
0:52Slides 11–14
Charter rounds		Round 1: reveal the slide, students copy the topic onto their worksheet, press Enter to start the 3:00 clock. Round 2 the same. Round 3 is on the paper already; 5:00 clock, teams talk. Round 4: 7:00 clock, teams write and sign. Photograph every signed page before anyone leaves the table. Close with: you just ran your first brain dump. This exact routine happens most class days, on anatomy.
1:15Slide 15 + live siteSwitch to the Course Page tab and walk it: Start Here, a module page, Mastery OS with one recall card, the grade calculator with fake scores. Back to slide 15 for the four bookmarks.
1:27Slide 16
Scavenger hunt		Press Enter for the 10:00 clock. Debrief by having a different team answer each item; correct grading and attendance answers immediately. Expected answers are in the deck's speaker notes and the plan.
1:42Slides 17–18How you use AI, how they should. Say the integrity stakes plainly, then the standing offer: ask me where the line is.
1:50Slide 19
Fact-check drill		15:00 clock. Push any team that finds all five correct toward exact values and edge cases. Debrief the pattern: AI is weakest exactly where precision matters most.
2:10Slides 20–21The deal, then leave the sources slide up while they pack. Tonight's homework is on slide 20: bookmark Start Here, set up accounts, begin Week 1 pre-work, gloves and lab coat by Week 2.

If the block runs short, the scavenger hunt and the fact-check drill are the two pieces that move cleanly to class two. The charter does not move; teams need it before the first TBL.

Keys you will actually press

Cheat sheet

→ or Space opens the next box, then advances the slide
← closes an open box, then goes back
A marks every box on the slide seen, so the next arrow advances
Esc closes a zoomed box; press again to leave present mode
T shows or hides the clock on any slide
Enter starts and pauses the clock
+ / − adds or removes 30 seconds, even while running
0 resets the clock; drag the clock face to move it

Activity slides preload their own time: 3:00 for rounds 1 and 2, 5:00 for round 3, 7:00 for round 4, 10:00 for the hunt, 15:00 for the fact-check drill. Digits turn gold in the last 30 seconds and read Pencils down at zero.

That evening

After class

  1. In Team Formation Engine, click Copy as text and paste the teams into your notes before closing the tab; nothing is saved once it closes.
  2. Create the Canvas groups for the section from that list, one group per park.
  3. File the charter photos by team name. You will point back to these at the first sign of friction.
  4. Collect and skim the worksheets: full credit for real effort on rounds 1 and 2. That is the first brain dump grade.
  5. Repeat the whole sheet for the next section, starting from the podium setup.
