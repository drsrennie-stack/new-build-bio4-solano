What is on each exam?

Each exam covers one module. Everything you can be asked is in your Competency Packet and your lab structure list. Nothing else is on the exam.

What happens on exam day?

  • Same day: lecture exam and lab practical.
  • Lecture exam: mostly apply and reason, not just recall.
  • Lab practical: timed stations. Spelling counts. Pen only.
  • Half of each practical is on the cadaver. On Exam 1 that half was microscope slides.
  • Doors lock when the practical starts.
  • Bonus questions are built in. No score counts above 102%.
