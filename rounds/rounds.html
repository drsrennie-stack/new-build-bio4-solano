Keyboard, screen reader, and speaking

Turn on keyboard targets with the keyboard button. Each structure region becomes a button you can reach with Tab and press with Enter. Regions are announced by letter so nothing is given away; in the recall round the letters are shuffled each pass.

The Say it button listens through your microphone and checks what you said. It works in Chrome, Edge, and Safari. The typed answer is what counts for spelling.

The red Record button saves your own voice saying the name. Your recordings stay on this device until you download them.
